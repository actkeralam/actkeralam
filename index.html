Who We Are

Empowering CS Teachers Across Keralam

The Association of Computer Science Teachers (ACT) is a group of Computer Science/ Computer Applications teachers working in Higher Secondary schools across Keralam. It provides a platform for teachers to come together, share ideas, and support each other in improving teaching and learning.

The association organizes training programs, workshops, and discussions to help teachers stay updated with new developments in Computer Science and teaching methods. It also helps in addressing common academic concerns and improving the quality of Computer Science education.

Through teamwork and sharing of knowledge, ACT aims to strengthen Computer Science education and benefit both teachers and students in Kerala.
